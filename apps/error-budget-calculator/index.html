Allowed downtime = window × (1 − target). A time-based budget assumes a steady request rate; for a request-ratio SLO, read the same numbers as a share of requests rather than minutes.

Burn rate = share of budget spent ÷ share of window elapsed. 1× spends the budget exactly by the window's end; 2× exhausts it at the halfway mark.

Burn-rate alert thresholds follow the multi-window approach in Google's SRE Workbook: a fast burn over a short window pages, a slow burn over a long one opens a ticket.
